Amount

manual

Capture the number only. Currency symbols and codes are ignored, so both Rs.2,050.00 and INR 2050 work.

Rs\.?\s*(?P<amount>[\d,]+(?:\.\d{1,2})?)
2,050.00 ×
INR\s*(?P<amount>[\d,]+(?:\.\d{1,2})?)
no match ×

Patterns are tried top to bottom. The first one that produces a value wins — later ones cover other mail shapes.

Why: six fields never stack. The rail shows which fields still have no pattern, and one field gets the full width plus its test. Cost: you click through fields (one extra click per field you edit).
